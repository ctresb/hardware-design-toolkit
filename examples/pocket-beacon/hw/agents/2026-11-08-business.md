## Toolkit bugs and gaps (not fixed here; skills/ was out of scope)

1. **compliance-planner rules:** UN 38.3 does not trigger for `battery.chemistry = "coin-cell"` although a CR2032 is a lithium-metal cell; no rule for EN IEC 62368-1 clause 4.8 (coin cells, EU/UK); no rule for DULT unwanted-tracking expectations on item finders. Worked around with `hw/compliance/rules.local.json` and `--rules`. The trait model allows one chemistry value; a coin cell is both "coin-cell" and "li-primary".
2. **compliance_matrix.py summary:** per-scope totals double-count rows shared by several markets (RoHS/REACH, nickel, RF exposure); the cross-market total had to be computed by hand.
3. **cost_model.py SVG:** the title does not state a conclusion and the recurring bars carry no value labels (CONVENTIONS chart rules).
4. **cost_model.py:** the "Landed" rows include the NRE spread; landed recurring cost per unit is not shown separately, so cost.md computes it by hand.
5. **cost_model.py:** regenerating overwrites `cost.md`, so the skill's instruction to "write cost.md around the script's tables" is lost on every re-run. A separate generated file (for example `cost/tables.md`) would keep the narrative.
6. **roadmap.py SVG:** stage labels are cut mid-word ("LaunchPa", "JLCPC"); worked around with shorter names and a `detail` field. It should ellipsize at a word boundary or wrap.
7. **roadmap.py long-lead check:** flags "LATE" from factory lead time even when the part is in stock; it has no field for stock on hand.
