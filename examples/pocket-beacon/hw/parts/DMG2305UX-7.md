# DMG2305UX-7: P-MOSFET for reverse-polarity protection (Diodes Inc.)

## Identity

- Manufacturer: Diodes Incorporated. Datasheet DS36196 Rev. 8-2, July 2021 [verified: diodes.com/assets/Datasheets/DMG2305UX.pdf, 2026-11-08].
- Ordering: `DMG2305UX-7` = 3,000 per 7-inch reel; `DMG2305UX-13` = 10,000 per 13-inch reel [verified: datasheet "Ordering Information"]. Marking `23X` + date code.

## Why this part

Q1 sits in the battery's negative or positive path with its gate on the opposite rail. With the cell reversed, the FET is off. With the cell correct, the FET is enhanced by the cell voltage. It must be fully on at the end-of-life cell voltage of 2.0 V (D-014).
