# ABS06-32.768KHZ-9-T: 32.768 kHz crystal

## Identity

- Manufacturer: Abracon.
- Full orderable MPN: `ABS06-32.768KHZ-9-T`. `-9` = CL 9 pF [verified: DigiKey and LCSC attributes, 2026-11-08]. `-T` = tape and reel [assumption: Abracon ABS06 ordering table not read in this session].

## Why this part

Stage 2 chose it for the BLE sleep clock (D-017). The CC2340 also has an internal LFOSC (standby 0.71 µA with LFOSC vs 0.74 µA with LFXT, SWRS272F §7.9). If the TI BLE stack accepts LFOSC as the sleep clock, Y2, C13 and C14 can go (Q-PB-102). That saves about USD 0.42 at 1k and about 6 mm² of board.

## Load capacitors (C13, C14)

[estimate] C = 2 × (CL − Cstray) = 2 × (9 − 2.5) ≈ 13 pF → 12 pF C0G is a sensible start. No internal LFXT capacitance is described in SWRS272F, so the external caps stay. Tune on EVT with the RTC drift measured over 24 h.
