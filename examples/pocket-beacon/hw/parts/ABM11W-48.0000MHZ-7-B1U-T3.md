# ABM11W-48.0000MHZ-7-B1U-T3: 48 MHz crystal (recommended Y1)

## Identity

- Manufacturer: Abracon, ABM11W series [verified: ABM11W datasheet, revised 10.06.2020].
- Suffix decode [verified: ABM11W options table]: `-7` = CL 7 pF; `B` = −20 °C to +70 °C; `1` = ±10 ppm tolerance; `U` = ±10 ppm stability (offered only for I, E and B temperature ranges); `T3` = 3,000 reel.

## Why this part

It resolves Q-PB-04. Same family and footprint as the stage-2 choice, which is out of stock. 7 pF is the CC2340 on-chip default load (SWRS272F §7.20.3.1 note 2). ±10 ppm + ±10 ppm sits well inside the BLE ±50 ppm budget. −20 °C to +70 °C covers REQ-014 (−10 °C to +45 °C use, −20 °C to +60 °C storage).
