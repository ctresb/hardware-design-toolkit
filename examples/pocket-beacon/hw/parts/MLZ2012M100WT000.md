# MLZ2012M100WT000: 10 µH multilayer inductor, 0805 (L1, U1 DC/DC)

## Identity

- TDK, MLZ series, MLZ2012 size, Commercial Grade. Status "Production" [verified: TDK product page via r.jina.ai, 2026-11-08].
- `T000` = embossed plastic tape, 180 mm reel, 2,000 per reel [verified: same].

## What the SoC requires

- TI states only "A 10µH and 10µF load capacitor are required on the VDDR voltage rail. They must be placed close to the DCDC output pin" [verified: TI SWRS272F rev F, §7.4 note 2]. The datasheet gives no DCR or saturation-current limit.
- TI's own LaunchPad (LP-EM-CC2340R53, MCU143A) uses **Murata LQM18DN100M70L**: 10 µH ±20 %, 0.1 A, 0603 [verified: TI SWRC397 design files, MCU143A_BOM.xlsx, 2026-11-08]. LCSC lists it with DCR 1.365 Ω, Isat 100 mA and rated current 300 mA [verified: LCSC product API C713281, 2026-11-08].

## Checks against the design

- **Inductance:** 10 µH, as TI requires. Met.
- **DCR:** 470 mΩ typ, about one third of TI's reference inductor (1.365 Ω). Lower conduction loss. Met.
- **Current:** the CC2340 DC/DC load is a few mA average and tens of mA peak [estimate: SWRS272F current tables, TX 5.1 mA at VDDS]. 150 mA at 50 % inductance drop leaves ample margin [estimate].
- **Height:** the BOM states 1.0 mm. The maximum is 1.45 mm, 0.45 mm taller. Re-check the board-side height budget (enclosure-engineer and pcb-engineer). If the 1.45 mm does not fit, use LQM18DN100M70L, which is 0603 and the TI reference part. Read its height from the Murata datasheet first; the Murata page returned "page not found".
