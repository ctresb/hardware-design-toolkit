# 3034TR: CR2032 ultra-low-profile SMD retainer (Keystone)

## Identity

- Manufacturer: Keystone Electronics.
- Catalog numbers [verified: Keystone catalog page 11 "Surface mount (SMT) coin cell retainers", K75p11.pdf, 2026-11-08]:
  - `3034` bulk, `3034TR` tape and reel: tin-nickel plate.
  - `3044` / `3044TR`: matte tin plate.
  - Reel: 44 mm tape, 24 mm pitch, 1000 per reel.
- Not in the BOM. Proposed as the replacement for BT1 (Q-PB-01).

## Why this part

Lowest-profile retainer found for a CR2032 at an authorized distributor. It keeps the cell user-replaceable without tools and leaves the PCB pad as the negative contact.
