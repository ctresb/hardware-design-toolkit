## Layout and matching (eval board)

[verified: datasheet p.3; the drawing is an image, values read from its labels]
- 50 Ω GCPW feed line with ground vias along it ("highly recommended").
- Ground clearance area around the antenna at the board corner. The drawing labels show 6.5 mm and 4.0 mm for the clearance, on a board 39.5 mm wide. [estimate: the exact clearance rectangle must be read from the drawing or Johanson's CAD before layout.]
- Eval-board matching network: 1.0 pF, 2.7 nH and 3.9 nH (Johanson parts QSCF500Q1R0B1GV001T, LRC0402CS2N7GV001T, LRC0402CS3N9GV001T).
- Johanson recommends leaving slots for a **tee (series-shunt-series)** network. Values on the product board will differ (layout, thickness, material), and are found by tuning.

## Checks against the design

- Size: 3.4 × 1.8 × 1.4 mm max. Update `size_max_mm` in the BOM.
- Placement: board corner, with copper clearance on every layer under and around it.
- Matching: the stage-2 BOM has a pi network (C11, L2, C12). Johanson asks for a tee. Lay out a 5-pad set that can be fitted as either pi or tee (Q-PB-03 update).
- Board size effect: the eval board is 39.5 mm wide. On a 28 mm board the efficiency will be lower [estimate]. Range is verified on EVT (REQ-010).
