# CR2032: 3 V lithium coin cell (Panasonic Energy)

## Identity

- Manufacturer: Panasonic Energy Co., Ltd. Datasheet "CR2032 CR Coin-Type Lithium Primary Battery", as of April 2022 [verified: Panasonic datasheet via api.pim.na.industrial.panasonic.com fileversion 250011, 2026-11-08].
- The BOM number `CR-2032/BN` is Panasonic's bulk export code. At DigiKey it appears only as `CR2032/BN` on the Marketplace (Dantona Industries), USD 9.75 at qty 1 [verified: DigiKey search, 2026-11-08]. The authorized stocked part is `CR2032` (DigiKey 11-CR2032-ND, tray). Use `CR2032`.

## Why this part

REQ: ≥ 12 months on a cell the user can buy anywhere. Branded cells matter: TI measured branded and no-name CR2032s under BLE-like pulses. Some vendors, including some branded ones, delivered only about 50% of rated capacity [verified: TI SWRA349 §3, 2010].

## Checks against the design

- Rail VBAT: 2.0 V to 3.3 V matches the curves. The ring in 3× mode is the binding case (see Power).
- Cold: at −10 °C the 1 kΩ curve already sits near 2.45 V at 50% depth. Firmware must fall back to 2× or 1× ring when VBAT under load drops (Q-PB-02).

## Risks

| Risk | Rating | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Users fit no-name cells (≈ 50% capacity) | high | battery-life claim states "branded cell"; low-battery warning at 2.3 V |
| Coin-cell ingestion: US and EU child-safety rules for coin-cell products | high | child-resistant lid (two independent movements or a tool), warning labels; compliance-planner confirms the rule set [assumption: rule names not verified here] |
| Loud ring browns out SoC at end of life | medium | ring mode chosen from loaded VBAT; bulk C1/C2 for TX only |
