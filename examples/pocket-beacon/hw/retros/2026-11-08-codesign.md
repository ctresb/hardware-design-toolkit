## Mistakes and causes
- Bayonet lid specified before checking the holder's 3D model; it does not fit 10.0 mm (L-510, D-511).
- The sound port was assumed central; the drawing puts it in a corner (L-511).
- Several rounds of routing, because the cell's 17.8 mm negative pad forbids non-GND vias under most of the board (D-517). Freerouting 2.5.0 stops early on this board (TB-8). Freerouting 1.9.0 under xvfb got furthest.
