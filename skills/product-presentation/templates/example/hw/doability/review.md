# Doability review: Fixture Tag (fictional example)

**Short answer:** go with changes. The design can be built with off-the-shelf parts. The coin cell needs a reservoir capacitor for the buzzer, and battery life must be measured before the enclosure is tooled.

See `score.md` for the verdict table and `findings.json` for every finding.

## Must be measured or quoted

1. Idle current on the dev kit, with advertising every 2 s and every 3 s
2. Buzzer burst on a CR2032 with the 220 µF reservoir: lowest VBAT at −10 °C
3. Enclosure set quote (shell, lid, O-ring) at 1k and 10k from two molders
4. IPX7 immersion on SLA prototypes with the acoustic membrane
5. Radio lab quotes for FCC, CE/RED and UKCA
